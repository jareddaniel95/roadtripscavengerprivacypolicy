Privacy Policy

Road Trip Scavenger · Effective October 10, 2026

Road Trip Scavenger (“the app”) is a scavenger hunt game for car passengers, made by Jared Daniel. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. The short version: the app does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not send any information about you or your device to us or to anyone else.

Information stored on your device

To keep your game going if you close the app, it saves the following on your phone only:

  • the current or most recent game (its start time, length, object list and game code),
  • which objects you checked off, and
  • that you confirmed you are a passenger, so the notice isn’t shown again.

This data never leaves your device. Deleting the app, or clearing its data in your phone’s settings, removes it.

Camera

If you choose “Scan QR Code,” the app asks for camera access to read a game’s QR code. The camera image is processed on your device only to find the code. No photos or video are saved, and nothing from the camera is sent anywhere. You can turn camera access off at any time in your phone’s settings and type game codes instead.

Sharing game codes

Game codes contain only the game’s start time, length and list of objects. When you share a code using your phone’s share sheet, it goes to the app or person you choose, under that app’s own privacy policy.

Crash reports from Apple and Google

If you have allowed your phone to share diagnostics with app developers, Apple or Google may give us anonymous crash and performance statistics through their developer tools. These do not identify you, and you can turn this sharing off in your phone’s settings.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13, because it does not collect personal information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, for example if a future version adds features that use data, the updated policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date before those changes take effect.
